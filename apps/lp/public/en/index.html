FAQ

Where can I use it?

The browser version needs no install. We have checked it on desktop Chrome, Firefox, and Safari, and on Chrome for Android. The apps are for Windows 10 / 11 and Linux (x86_64); there are no macOS, iOS, or Android apps. Some features, such as full device backup, are app-only.

What do I need to get started?

First, confirm that you are 18 or older and accept the app terms. No network connection starts until you accept. Community Nodes are used only after you read and accept their documents.

Where is my account stored?

The key that identifies your account is stored only on your device (in the browser, for the browser version). There is no central recovery, so export the key, encrypted, from Settings and keep it safe.
